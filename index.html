About us

Who we are

The MYKNXNS Community Development Organization (C.D.O.) is the charitable arm of MYKNXNS Inc., created to celebrate the extraordinary value of everyday people by preserving their stories, strengthening communities, and passing forward the wisdom that can help future generations build stronger families, more resilient communities, and a better future.

We created the MYKNXNS C.D.O. to extend our mission of preservation, connection, and community impact beyond the platform and directly into the communities we serve. At the heart of our work is a simple belief: one of the world's greatest untapped resources is the collective wisdom of everyday people.

We work alongside individuals, families, and communities to preserve the stories, traditions, experiences, knowledge, and life lessons that might otherwise be lost. At the same time, we invest in people and communities today: helping create opportunities, strengthen connections, and provide support when it is needed most.

Through scholarships, educational support and tutoring, community investment, community partnerships, and access to legacy-preservation resources, we seek to ensure that what one generation has learned can become a resource for the next.

Our vision is to inspire a global movement that preserves culture, strengthens communities, celebrates the extraordinary value of everyday lives, and ensures the wisdom of today empowers the generations of tomorrow.

What guides us

Our core values

Every Story Matters

We believe every person carries wisdom, experiences, and relationships that have the power to inspire, teach, and transform others. Every life has value, and every story deserves to be heard and preserved.

Guiding principle: We honor the extraordinary value found in ordinary lives.

Preserve with Purpose

We are committed to protecting the stories, lessons, and relationships that might otherwise be lost to time. We preserve not simply to remember the past, but to equip future generations with the wisdom they need to build a better future.

Guiding principle: Today's experiences become tomorrow's wisdom.

Impact in Action

We transform purpose into measurable action through community collaboration, innovation, service, and investment, creating a lasting social impact.

Guiding principle: We leave every community stronger than we found it.

Communities Hold the Answers

We believe the greatest wisdom, solutions, and opportunities already exist within our communities. We empower communities to preserve their existing knowledge, strengthen relationships, and pass forward a legacy that inspires future generations.

Guiding principle: Our role is to listen, preserve, connect, and empower.

Leadership

Meet the people behind the mission

Jay Johnson

Founder

“When an elder dies, a library burns.”— African proverb

Jay Johnson embodies this philosophy through MYKNXNS, which he founded with a simple belief that everyday people carry extraordinary stories, wisdom, experiences, and knowledge worth preserving.

His work is dedicated to turning that belief into action: Preserving what matters, strengthening communities, creating opportunities, and ensuring that what one generation has learned can empower the next.

Muhammad Ali once said, “Service to others is the rent you pay for your room here on Earth.”

Jay believes his purpose is to serve humanity by helping preserve the knowledge, wisdom, experiences, and perspectives people accumulate throughout their lives. In turn, they can continue to teach, inspire, and empower others long after the moment has passed.

Through MYKNXNS, he hopes fewer of our human libraries will be lost to time, and more of what humanity has learned will be passed forward.

We are all called to serve in different ways. Jay found his calling in serving humanity.
